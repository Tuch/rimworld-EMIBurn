[h1]EMIBurn[/h1]

[b]Changes what a solar flare does to your colony.[/b]

In vanilla, a solar flare simply shuts all electricity off until it passes. EMIBurn replaces that: the power keeps running, but your energized devices can [b]overheat and burst into flame[/b]. To stay safe you now have to [i]manually[/i] switch off power to anything you don't want burning.

[h1]How it works[/h1]
[list]
[*]During a solar flare, electricity is [b]no longer disabled[/b] — everything stays powered.
[*]While the flare is active, powered devices have a chance to overheat and catch fire.
[*]The fire is a small flame burst: it damages the device and can ignite anything flammable around it (wooden walls, furniture, chemfuel, etc.).
[*]You survive by flicking off power to the devices you want to protect.
[/list]

[h1]Generators are safe[/h1]
Power [b]generators[/b] — solar panels, geothermal, wind, and modded ones — are never damaged. They're the source you can't switch off to protect, so EMI leaves them alone. Only powered [i]consumers[/i] are at risk.

[h1]Settings[/h1]
Configurable in [b]Options → Mod settings → EMIBurn[/b] (after loading a game):
[list]
[*][b]Fire chance (%)[/b] — chance per device, per interval, to burst into flame during a flare.
[*][b]Min / Max interval[/b] — each fire check waits a random amount of time within this range.
[*][b]Show fire notifications[/b] — get a threat alert when a device ignites.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]RimWorld [b]1.6[/b].
[*]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url].
[*]Safe to add to an existing save. Works with modded generators and modded powered devices.
[/list]
